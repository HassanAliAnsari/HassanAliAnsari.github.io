TRATON AB2025–PRESENT

Camera-based 3D perception

Developing detection and tracking models for driver assistance, including extending a perception model to predict lane markings in 3D.

My contribution

I translate driving-feature requirements into perception tasks and define evaluation metrics with simulation engineers. I optimize and quantize models, convert them from ONNX to QNN, and validate inference on Qualcomm hardware with platform engineers.

I also contributed to discussions on technical feasibility, perception architecture and the hardware compute budget for an L2++ ADAS production project.

3D detectionQuantizationQualcomm QNN
SCANIA CV AB2022–2025

Real-time occupancy inference

Taking 3D occupancy models from Python and PyTorch development to a C++ inference application on NVIDIA hardware.

My contribution

I developed models through training and evaluation, optimized inference with ONNX, TensorRT and custom CUDA kernels, and wrote the C++ application that ran them. The work focused on meeting real-time compute and latency constraints.

C++TensorRTCUDA
SCANIA CV AB2022–2025

Tracking across time

Moving camera-based 2D multi-object tracking from Kalman filtering to an end-to-end trainable transformer tracker.

My contribution

In related 3D perception work, I integrated a trainable tracker with an object detector and used its tracks to develop downstream motion forecasting and occupancy prediction models. I also built prototypes to compare methods and guide technology selection.

ABOUT ME

Vision software.
Engineering perspective.

I’m Hassan, a computer vision engineer working across image analysis, video understanding, and applied perception. My background combines a bachelor’s in Electrical Engineering with an Erasmus Mundus joint master’s in Image Processing and Computer Vision.

I enjoy comparing approaches, building prototypes and working through the details that make a model useful. Alongside engineering, I have supervised master’s thesis projects and served as Scrum Master.

My broader applied ML experience includes LLMs and vision-language models for video tagging, some hands-on work with RAG, AWS EC2, SageMaker inference and SQL in Databricks for comparing auto-labeled data and model predictions.
